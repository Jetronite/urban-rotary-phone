**Best for:** General NOUN groups, Faculty of Science, Computer Science groups.

```text

Please do not make the mistake of memorizing past question answer keys (e.g. "Question 14 is Option C").

In the actual CBT e-exam, the NOUN examination algorithm frequently changes the coefficients or rephrases the equations directly from the official e-courseware. If you don't understand the step-by-step calculation, you will get trapped.

Look at this common problem from Module 1, Unit 2 (Quadratic Equations & Roots):
--------------------------------------------------
[QUESTION]
If α and β are the roots of the equation 2x² - 5x + 3 = 0, find the value of (1/α + 1/β).

❌ THE COMMON MISTAKE:
Most students waste 3 minutes trying to find the individual roots using the quadratic formula, get stuck with fractions, panic under the exam timer, and guess Option A.

✅ THE 30-SECOND EXAM TRICK:
1. Remember that (1/α + 1/β) = (α + β) / (αβ)
2. From the equation ax² + bx + c = 0:
  - Sum of roots (α + β) = -b/a = -(-5)/2 = 5/2
  - Product of roots (αβ) = c/a = 3/2
3. Therefore: (1/α + 1/β) = (5/2) / (3/2) = 5/3.
--------------------------------------------------

Notice that you never needed to solve for x!

We have compiled over 250 of these exact courseware calculation traps with full, step-by-step LaTeX worked solutions into a timed CBT exam simulator.

👉 You can test your speed and attempt 15 diagnostic questions for free here:
https://jaypq.com/subjects

📲 NOTE FOR ANDROID USERS: If you are reading this inside the Facebook app, tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top right and select "Open in Chrome / External Browser" so the interactive math equations display smoothly.

Drop a comment below with your major/department if you want more worked examples for MTH102 and PHY101! 👇
```
